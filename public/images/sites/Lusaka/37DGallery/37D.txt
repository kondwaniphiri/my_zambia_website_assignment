37d Gallery is a dynamic art space nestled in Lusaka, Zambia. This contemporary gallery showcases the rich tapestry of Zambian artistic talent, offering a platform for both established and emerging artists to exhibit their work.

Beyond being a showcase for art, 37d Gallery is deeply committed to the community. As a non-profit organization, it supports the stART Foundation, which focuses on arts education and outreach programs. Visitors can immerse themselves in a world of color and creativity while contributing to a worthy cause.

The gallery boasts a diverse collection of paintings, sculptures, photographs, and ceramics. In addition to art, you can explore the gallery's gem shop or relax at The Rock Cafe while appreciating the stunning artworks around you.

37d Gallery is more than just an art space; it's a cultural hub that celebrates Zambian creativity and contributes to the growth of the local art scene.
