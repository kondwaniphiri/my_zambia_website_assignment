Muka Munya Club & The Monkey Pools offer a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of Lusaka. Nestled amidst lush greenery, this exclusive retreat provides a perfect blend of relaxation and recreation.

The Muka Munya Club is a member-only facility that boasts a range of amenities to cater to diverse interests. From swimming pools and tennis courts to a clubhouse for socializing, there's something for everyone. The club's ambiance is ideal for unwinding and connecting with friends and family.

Just a short distance away, The Monkey Pools offer a refreshing natural experience. These crystal-clear pools, fed by the Muka Munya River, invite visitors to take a dip in nature's embrace. Surrounded by verdant forest, the pools provide a tranquil setting for picnics, swimming, and simply enjoying the outdoors.

Whether you're a club member seeking leisure or a nature enthusiast looking for a refreshing escape, Muka Munya Club & The Monkey Pools offer an idyllic oasis within the city.
