Lower Zambezi National Park is a pristine wilderness located in southern Zambia, bordering Zimbabwe's Mana Pools National Park. Renowned for its abundant wildlife and breathtaking natural beauty, the park offers an unparalleled safari experience.

Characterized by a stunning landscape of rolling hills, open plains, and lush riverine forests, the park is home to a diverse array of animals, including large herds of elephants, buffalo, and hippos. Leopards, lions, and crocodiles are also prevalent, making it a thrilling destination for wildlife enthusiasts.

The Zambezi River, which flows through the heart of the park, is a major attraction, offering opportunities for boating, fishing, and canoeing. The riverbanks are teeming with life, and visitors can often spot a variety of birds, including the iconic African fish eagle.

With its remote location and limited infrastructure, Lower Zambezi offers a truly authentic and exclusive safari experience. It's a place where travelers can immerse themselves in nature, encounter wildlife up close, and create unforgettable memories.
